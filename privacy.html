Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Client-Side Execution & Zero Data Collection

SearchPro operates 100% on the client side in your web browser. We do not store, collect, transmit, or monetize your search terms, search history, or personal IP addresses on any backend servers.

2. LocalStorage Usage

We utilize your browser's local storage (localStorage) exclusively to store user preferences such as Dark/Light theme mode and optional search history for your convenience. This data remains on your local device at all times.

3. Third-Party Search Engines

When you execute a search, SearchPro generates search queries that open directly in your chosen search provider (e.g., Google). Your interaction with third-party search engines is governed by their respective privacy policies.
